## What interacts most (the research, in one paragraph)

The topics that interact most are **body-and-daily-moment anchors a reader can self-verify in 2 seconds while scrolling**, framed as *"why most people feel X after Y"* (never *"is YOUR X a sign of…"*). Two levers fire across every audience: **self-recognition** ("that's literally my body / my afternoon / my husband") and a **calm, positive, normalizing payoff**. Self-recognition is the strongest **tag** driver for the core women-35-65 segment; curiosity-gap "body-signal" and "myth-correction" framings earn the **click** (the revenue lever) by withholding one specific answer. Winning domains hit 3-4 segments at once: **sleep (strongest), gut/digestion, energy/fatigue, joints/back stiffness, cold hands/swollen feet.** Positive mild-awe beats negative for sharing; fear, clinical tone, named conditions, and invented stats both suppress reach and break the guardrails. The safest high-reach play: closed-loop, calm "good to know" facts that make the sharer look helpful and the tagged friend feel seen.
