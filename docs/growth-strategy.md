## 1. The thesis, in plain language

**You do not need followers. You need an engine that turns one month of articles into a month of cross-channel curiosity bait — and then you need to point that bait at the surfaces that reliably hand reach to a no-name account *and* send readers who actually read more than one page.**

Here's the instinct you had, answered straight: **yes, some platforms genuinely give brand-new accounts real organic reach — but they are not all the same, and most of the ones that do are *terrible* at sending engaged clicks to a display-ad blog.** The mechanism is well-documented across these platforms' own creator guidance (verify against current docs, but the direction is stable):

- **Why they do it.** TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, and Pinterest all rank *content*, not *creators*. Every new post starts in a small test pool matched to your topic by caption/OCR/transcript/keywords, and if early watchers complete, save, or share it, it graduates to a bigger pool. Follower count and account age weigh far less than that early-engagement signal. They do this on purpose: short-video apps compete for a constant firehose of fresh clips, and Pinterest's entire value proposition is *fresh, search-aligned ideas* — so all of them are structurally biased to give unknowns a shot.
- **The trap.** The platforms that hand out the *most* cold-start reach (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) are *engineered to keep people inside the app*. TikTok, Instagram, and Shorts all suppress or forbid off-platform links in captions/comments and demote content that pushes users away. So the reach is real, but it converts to **swiping eyeballs, not clicking readers** — and a bouncing single-page tap is worth almost nothing in a display-ad model.

**The honest map of "hands-out-reach" vs. "sends-engaged-clicks"** (directional, based on each channel's known link behavior and typical visit quality — confirm the specifics before betting a session on any single cell):

| Surface | Gives a new account reach? | Sends *engaged, multi-page* clicks? |
|---|---|---|
| **Facebook *Groups* (you moderate)** | N/A — already warm | ✅ Best proven engaged source |
| **Pinterest** | ✅ Yes, zero followers needed | ✅ Best *quality* click channel (research-mode, long sessions) |
| **Google Discover** | ✅ Yes, indexed = eligible, freshness-favored | ✅ Interest-matched mobile readers |
| **Facebook *Reels*** | ✅ Yes (the cold-start surface FB still has) | ❌ Top-of-funnel only — grows the group, doesn't click |
| **TikTok / IG Reels / Shorts** | ✅ Yes (TikTok friendliest) | ❌ Link-suppressed by design |
| **Reddit / Quora** | ⚠️ Gated by trust, not algorithm | ✅ Highest quality-per-visit, but low/lumpy volume |

**So the strategy writes itself: ride the reach-givers that *also* send readers (Pinterest, Discover), keep your proven home base (the groups) as the conversion engine, and treat the link-suppressed video surfaces as a *funnel into the groups and the brand* — never as a direct traffic bet.** The ethical way to "ride the algorithm" on every one of these is identical to your existing guardrail: completion-rate-winning, genuinely-useful, general-health-facts content. The named policies that *bury* sensational/miracle/personal-diagnosis content — **Pinterest's health-misinformation policy, YouTube's medical-misinformation policy, Meta's health/misinformation classifier, TikTok's integrity/fact-checking policy** — are the same ones you're already designed to clear. **Your brand guardrail and the reach algorithm point the same direction.** That is the unfair advantage. (Name-checking those policies isn't decoration: it lets a future audit re-verify each platform claim against the live policy, since they update often.)

## 2. Why every recommendation ties back to the formula

> **Monthly revenue = link clicks × pages per session × (RPM ÷ 1000)**

A channel is only worth your scarce monthly hours if it moves a term in that formula *in the right direction*. The display-ad model punishes raw taps and rewards **engaged, multi-page readers** — because pages/session multiplies clicks *and* lifts effective RPM (more impressions per reader; and search/social surfaces tend to reward "quality destinations" where people stay, with more future reach — so a deep reader pays you twice).

That single fact re-ranks everything:

- A **Pinterest** click (low bounce, long research-mode sessions) tends to be worth more per visit than a Facebook-group click, which is worth far more than a **Reel/TikTok** tap (low-intent, bounce-prone).
- So the strategy **over-weights the channels that send fewer-but-deeper readers** and **refuses to treat link-suppressed video as a traffic channel at all** — video's job is to *feed* the deep-reader channels (grow the group, grow the brand, grow in-app search presence), measured on group joins and brand searches, **never on Reel views**.

Every tier below is sorted by *expected lift to clicks × pages/session*, discounted by *effort* and *time-to-payoff* — not by vanity reach.

## 3. The priority tiers (do NOT do all of these at once)

> A solo operator with one session a month who lights up six channels lights up none of them. The tiers are a *sequence*, not a menu.

### 🥇 TIER 1 — Do now (the home base + the one compounding click channel)

**1A. Facebook moderated Groups — your proven engaged-traffic engine. Keep it; tighten it.**
This is the only channel already sending warm, multi-page readers. It bypasses the new-account reach problem entirely (Groups still out-distribute Pages). Nothing outranks protecting and optimizing it.

**1B. Pinterest — seed it *this month*, because it's slow and it compounds.**
It is the single **best-quality** organic click channel for a display-ad blog (research-mode readers, the highest pages/session of any cold channel), and it hands reach to zero-follower accounts. It also offers a **native scheduled queue inside its own product**, used on human-reviewed pins — a real efficiency win, *within the limits described in 4/1B* (it is **not** an unattended bulk-automation drip; see the correction there). The catch is it's slow (real clicks typically build over the first several months — verify the current curve), which is *exactly why you plant it now* and let it grow while the groups carry today's revenue.

> **Why these two first:** 1A is the only thing already paying you. 1B is the highest-quality *new* click source and the one new channel where a single session's reviewed output can be partly queued rather than hand-posted one at a time. Together they need almost no new infrastructure (the bundle already emits Pinterest fields), and they're the two that send the *engaged multi-page reader the RPM model is built on*.

### 🥈 TIER 2 — Next (the reach funnel + the near-free byproduct)

**2A. Facebook Reels — the cold-start *funnel* into your groups.**
The surface that gives a near-zero-follower FB presence real discovery reach (a large and growing share of the feed is now unconnected content; completion-rate ranking lets a new account out-distribute a big one — verify current magnitudes). **It does not send engaged clicks — it grows group/Page membership**, which feeds 1A. Add it once the Tier-1 batch is humming, because it slots into the *same* monthly render pass.

**2B. Google Discover — the compounding, near-free byproduct of articles you already write.**
Not authority-gated like classic Search; freshness-favored, so a new site can be *tested within weeks* (verify the current freshness window). Recent Discover guidance has emphasized **US-publisher relevance** and **topic-cluster expertise** — both tailwinds for a focused US "why does my body…" site (treat the specific dates/labels as fast-moving; confirm before relying on them). It sends curious, interest-matched mobile readers — good pages/session if your clusters interlink. Costs you two new bundle fields, not a new channel.

### 🥉 TIER 3 — Later / optional (quality diversification + brand reach experiments)

**3A. Reddit + Quora — best *quality-per-visit* of any channel, but low/lumpy volume and a multi-week trust warm-up.** Worth it *only* once Tier 1–2 are stable, because a single helpful comment can keep ranking in Google for a long time (Reddit has become one of the most-surfaced sites in Google results — directional; verify). **These are net-new free-form copy surfaces with higher personal-advice drift risk; they ship only after the gate is extended to cover them (see §5 and §7).**

**3B. TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts — brand + in-app-search reach, *not* a traffic bet.** These are link-suppressed by design. Run them only as a *zero-marginal-effort repurpose lane* off the video you're already rendering for FB Reels, and judge them on brand-search lift, never on clicks. Demote to "repurpose-only" the moment GA4 shows the bio/description links send no engaged sessions.

For each: **what to post · winning format · cadence · how Claude + the engine + Higgsfield produce it, review-first.** The thread running through all of them: *one article → a multi-channel creative pack, every asset through the same `/guardrail-audit` gate (extended to cover the new fields before they ship — see §5), every link UTM-tagged, every human step kept human.*

### 🥇 1A · Facebook Groups (the conversion engine)

**The play.** Post like a real member, never like a brand. The article link goes in the **first comment, never the caption** (link-in-caption meaningfully suppresses reach vs. link-in-first-comment; Meta's own guidance has endorsed link-in-comments — verify current behavior at session start). Between article drops, raise the group's Meaningful Social Interaction so the *whole group's* reach lifts and members are warm before the next link.

**Winning formats:**
- A native text/photo post written as a member ("Most people don't realize…"), link in first comment.
- **Save-worthy reference posts** — simple general-fact checklists (saves are a strong quality signal).
- **One weekly engagement thread per A-tier group** — a poll or a "why does your body…" question.
- **One pinned evergreen** in an A-tier group, refreshed weekly (earns clicks all week with no new posting).

**Example, Dr Purg Jr. voice — group post (no link in caption):**
> *Funny thing most people never think about: you're actually a little taller first thing in the morning than at night. Not magic — it's what your spine does while you sleep. Wrote up the calm version of why. 👇*
> *(link in first comment)*

**Cadence.** One link per group every few days, **staggered over 3–5 days per article**, logged in the Cockpit. Never same-hour blast across groups. Reply to comments early where feasible (early replies correlate with more engagement and unlock non-follower reach — directional).

**How the engine produces it (already built).** `/draft-month` emits `facebook_hook`, `facebook_summary`, `facebook_first_comment` (with the URL), `overlay_text`, `bottom_hint_text`, and multiple hook variants per article — all through `/guardrail-audit`. The plugin renders the 1080×1350 card locally and builds **UTM links per hook-variant and per-group** so `/retro` can crown the winning angle. **Manual human steps (guardrail): you publish, and you post to the groups by hand.**

### 🥇 1B · Pinterest (the best-quality compounding click channel)

**The play.** Stand up a tightly-themed **business account** (for analytics + the native scheduled queue + RSS), claim the domain, and build keyword-named boards ("Why Does My Body Do That," "Everyday Health Facts," "Sleep & Your Body"). Then turn **each article into a small *set* of distinct pins**, each with a *different keyword angle* — because each distinct image+keyword is treated as a fresh pin and chases a separate search. One article, several search intents, modest marginal cost — *but every pin still passes the human review gate and the anti-slop bar, so volume stays deliberate.*

**Winning format:** **static 2:3 vertical image pins (1000×1500)** with bold readable overlay text stating the curiosity hook. Static image pins substantially outperform Idea/multi-slide pins for outbound clicks, and they carry the link (Idea/multi-slide pins generally *can't* link out — skip them). Use video pins sparingly (awareness only; they convert poorly to clicks). *(The "static outperforms Idea pins for clicks" point is directional from creator analyses — verify, but the link-out mechanic is the load-bearing reason and is structural.)*

**Example pin set from one article ("Why you're taller in the morning"):**
- Pin A overlay: **"Why you're taller in the morning"** → board *Why Does My Body Do That*
- Pin B overlay: **"What your spine does while you sleep"** → board *Sleep & Your Body*
- Pin C overlay: **"The height you lose by bedtime (and why)"** → board *Everyday Health Facts*

**CORRECTED scope — what is actually compliant and supported today (this replaces the earlier "auto-drip 150–250 pins" plan):**
1. **Pin *creation* is automated** — Higgsfield stills + bundle copy produce *drafts*. That part is real and cheap.
2. **Publishing is NOT unattended automation.** Per the engine's own plugin reference (`docs/social-syndicator.md`): *"publishing is a deliberate click on reviewed content; nothing is posted automatically."* Pinterest's **native in-product scheduler supports only a limited queue** — schedule what it allows; **manually publish the rest.** Higher-volume scheduling would require the **approved Pinterest API path** (`pins:write` scope, Pinterest app approval), which the plugin documents as still a deliberate, human-reviewed publish — not an unattended bulk drip. **Do not route 150–250 pins through any bulk auto-poster; that drifts toward the exact bulk-automation pattern the no-ToS-evasion guardrail forbids elsewhere.**
3. **Right-sized volume: ~30–60 *reviewed* pins per month** (not 150–250), so every pin clears the human review gate and the anti-slop bar. At ~10–15 articles × 3–5 angles, trim to the strongest, distinct designs.
4. **Cadence:** queue what the native scheduler permits to spread a few pins/day; publish the remainder by hand on your normal cadence. Re-pin the same URL no more than ~every 2 days, to ≤10 boards. **Verify the scheduler's current per-account queue limit at the start of each session** (it changes).

**How the engine produces it.** The bundle *already* emits `pinterest_title` (<90c), `pinterest_description` (<420c, no URL), and `pinterest_alt_text` (<125c). **A `pin_set[]` array (3–5 angle variants) is a proposed *plugin-side* contract change — it must land in the importer + Master Bundle Prompt + the gate's allowed-keys list FIRST (see §5); until then the extra angles ride in a companion file, not as extra bundle keys.** Higgsfield (`higgsfield-product-photoshoot`, `moodboard_pin` mode, or `higgsfield-generate` GPT Image 2) renders the 2:3 stills from the bundle's `image_prompt` + each overlay. **Anti-slop is non-negotiable here:** Pinterest's Gen-AI labeling/down-ranking targets content-farm AI pins — make each design *genuinely distinct and useful*, never one template stamped dozens of times (the ~30–60 cap enforces this). **Manual human step: you review the pin set, then publish/queue each one yourself.**

### 🥈 2A · Facebook Reels (the cold-start funnel into the groups)

**The play.** Use Reels for the *only* thing they're good at — cold-start discovery that **grows group/Page membership**. Every Reel ends by routing the viewer *into the warm group*, where the link-in-comment lives. You are not trying to send a Reel viewer to the site; you're turning a swiper into a group member who will click *next month's* article.

**Winning format:** **vertical Reel, 12–20s, single curiosity open-loop, hook in the first 1–2 seconds, captioned/silent-readable, payoff *before* the halfway mark.** Completion rate is the dominant ranking signal — a short clip people finish out-distributes a long one they swipe. End on a Page-follow / "full breakdown's in the group" CTA, **not** a one-tap site link.

**Example 15s Reel script, Dr Purg Jr. voice:**
> **[0–2s, on-screen, frame 1]** "You shrink a little every single day."
> **[2–8s]** "By tonight you'll be a touch shorter than you were at breakfast — and it's not your imagination."
> **[8–13s]** "It's just what the discs in your spine do under a day of gravity. They bounce back overnight."
> **[13–15s CTA]** "Follow for the calm version of why your body does the weird stuff."

**Cadence.** Consistency beats volume. Batch-render **12–16 Reels in the monthly session**, hand-upload **~3/week** spread across days. Reply to comments early where you can.

**How the engine produces it.** `reel_script` (12–20s: hook + 2–3 caption beats + group/Page CTA) is a **proposed new bundle field** — it ships only after the gate is extended with a **format-specific sub-check** (spoken + on-screen lines held to the same banned-phrase / general-only list as `overlay_text`; see §5) and the contract is updated plugin-side. Higgsfield renders it: text-to-image silent-hook card (`higgsfield-generate`) → **image-to-video / Seedance 2.0** animates a captioned vertical Reel → **Virality Predictor (`brain_activity`)** scores hook/retention/distraction *before* a human posts, so only high-completion clips ship. **Manual human steps: you review, you upload the Reel, you post the group link.**
**KPI discipline (with an honest measurement caveat):** the *goal* is Reel-driven group/Page growth, not Reel views. **The engine does not yet auto-attribute "joins caused by Reels."** Measure it with a manual proxy: a **Reels-specific landing CTA** (e.g., a dedicated group-join prompt or tagged entry point used only in Reels) and/or a **noted membership delta on Reel-posting days**, recorded by hand. If you want true attribution, that's a small future plugin addition — until then, treat the Reels→joins number as **hand-estimated, not tool-read.**

### 🥈 2B · Google Discover (the compounding byproduct)

**The play.** Make Discover a *byproduct* of the articles you already publish. Pick **1–2 tight topic clusters per month** (5–10 interlinked body-signal explainers each), give every article a **dominant 1200px+ 16:9 hero image**, an **honest, essence-capturing title** (separate from the FB curiosity hook!), a **real byline + date**, and **drip-publish 2–3/week** so each URL gets its fresh Discover test window (verify the current window length).

**The one trap to design around:** Discover penalizes **withheld-info / curiosity-gap headlines** — the exact pattern your FB hook relies on. **So the bundle must carry both:** a curiosity `facebook_hook` for the groups **and** a separate honest `seo_title` for the page. **This is a HARD rule, enforced in the extended gate: the published page title must be the honest `seo_title`, never the `facebook_hook`.** Using the FB hook as the page title is the single most likely reason Discover would filter these posts — and it's a brand-honesty rule as much as a reach rule.

**Winning format:** single curiosity-explainer article, one 16:9 hero (the image *is* the Discover card — "no image, no card"), `max-image-preview:large`, interlinked into its cluster (3–5 contextual links per article — which *also* lifts pages/session).

**Cadence.** ~2–3 articles/week per cluster, drip-scheduled in WordPress from the one monthly `/draft-month` run. Expect first test-impressions in weeks; trust compounds over several months (verify current timelines).

**How the engine produces it.** Extend the contract with an honest `seo_title` (already a known field/limit in the gate: `<58`) + a `discover_image_prompt` (1200×675) — **landing these plugin-side per §5 before emitting them.** Have `/draft-month` **plan each batch as an interlinked cluster** (it knows all sibling slugs in one run, so it can wire the internal links). Add a Discover sub-check to `/guardrail-audit`: **title-honesty (page title == `seo_title`, never the FB hook), image present, byline + date present.** Higgsfield renders the relatable, non-clinical 16:9 hero.
**Day-0 blocker (do this before any Discover push):** stand up the **real-author bio + "general information, not medical advice" editorial page**. This is E-E-A-T table stakes for YMYL health content *and* required by your own no-fake-doctor rule (never imply a credential you don't hold). Without it you risk both Discover suppression and crossing a guardrail. **Feed the GSC Discover report into `/retro`** so it crowns winning clusters and re-seeds `/find-topics`. (Note: Discover impressions/CTR are read from GSC; pages/session for those readers is read manually from GA4 — see §6's measurement labels.)

### 🥉 3A · Reddit + Quora (quality diversification, later — gate-extended first)

**The play.** No algorithm hands you reach here — *community trust* does. Run a **3–4 week comment-only warm-up** on 3–5 *non-clinical* curiosity subs (sleep, hydration, nutrition-curiosity, ELI5 body questions — **never** r/AskDocs or acute-medical-advice subs), being genuinely useful with zero links, to clear age + karma gates. Then post the *answer*, link only when it truly adds depth.

**The hard part the auditor caught — and the fix.** A Reddit reply to a specific OP's "why does *my* body do X" pulls naturally toward second-person diagnosis ("sounds like *you* might have…") — the single framing the body-signal rule bans. **So a `community_answer` field does NOT ship on "same gate" hand-waving. The canonical gate must first be extended with a format-specific sub-check for it:**
- Must answer **generally** ("why *most people* notice X"), never the OP's personal situation.
- **Banned:** any "you might have / sounds like / in your case" diagnostic framing.
- **Required:** a calm general note that *"a clinician is the right person for your specific case."*
- **Link optional**, and must respect each subreddit's self-promo ratio.

Only once that sub-check exists in `.claude/commands/guardrail-audit.md` is "the gate covers this field" *true by code* — then it can be claimed.

**Winning format:** the **helpful long comment** — answer the OP's "why does my body X" fully *and generally* in the comment, then optionally "I wrote a longer general breakdown if useful" with the link. On Quora: 200–300-word value-first answers. A good answer can keep surfacing in Google / AI Overviews for a long time (directional — verify).

**Cadence.** First month: 15–20 min/day comment warm-up. Steady state: ~one monthly session to post 2–4 native answers (one per flagship article) across Reddit + Quora, plus a few minutes/week of organic commenting to stay well inside each community's non-promo ratio. **Never batch-blast the same link across subs in a day — fastest ban there is.**

**How the engine produces it.** Add a gate-extended `community_answer` field (Reddit comment + Quora answer versions), link-optional. `/find-topics` mines *public* Reddit/Quora "why does my body" phrasing as a demand signal (**public-page reading only — no scraping, no login**). Higgsfield *image* (not video) makes native body-mechanism infographics for image-friendly subs. **Every post is a manual human action — Claude drafts, you review and post.**

### 🥉 3B · TikTok / IG Reels / YouTube Shorts (brand reach, repurpose-only)

**The play.** These are **link-suppressed by design** — treat them as a *brand + in-app-search* surface, not a traffic channel. The honest win: a large share of younger users search health questions *in-app on TikTok* (directional — verify), and OCR + transcript + caption keywords make explainer clips *rank in-app* for evergreen queries long after posting. Repurpose the *exact same vertical clip* you already rendered for FB Reels to all three (more reach, same effort).

**Winning format:** 7–15s "why does your body do X" with a question hook in the first ~2s and bold OCR-readable on-screen text. Optimize captions for in-app search (target keyword early in the caption). **Caption keyword sub-check (gate-enforced):** plain topic keywords are fine; **symptom-as-self-diagnosis phrasing is not** ("why does *my* X mean…" is banned exactly as in the body-signal rule).

**Cadence.** Hand-upload the FB-Reels batch to TikTok + Reels + Shorts on a 3–4/week drip, spread out. **Keep it light until GA4 proves anything.**

**How the engine produces it.** Zero new work beyond the (gate-extended) `reel_script` + the rendered clip. **AI-disclosure label on every upload** (TikTok and Meta AI-content disclosure are treated as hard requirements — confirm the current flow each session). **Manual upload, AI label applied by hand, review-first.** **Judge it in `/retro` on brand-search lift + any bio-link pages/session — and demote to repurpose-only the moment the funnel reads dry.**

## 5. The unified production system: one session → one month of cross-channel creative

This is the heart of it. **One focused Claude Code session a month fans one month of articles into a month of cross-channel creative, all human-reviewed before anything goes live.** The factory already exists; you're adding a few **draft-only bundle fields** and one render-and-score pass — *but only after a one-time plugin/contract update lands.*

**CRITICAL sequencing — the contract change is NOT free, and it must land before `/draft-month` emits the new fields.** The "New from Claude" importer and the canonical gate both enforce **"exactly the bundle keys, no extras"** as a *hard* check (the importer truncates silently; the gate rejects). So adding `pin_set[]`, `reel_script`, `seo_title`, `discover_image_prompt`, and `community_answer` to the same JSON object **will fail or be silently dropped until the plugin is updated.** Therefore:

1. **FIRST (one-time, Day 0–30 plugin work):** update **(a)** the importer's allowed keys, **(b)** the Master Bundle Prompt (`docs/chatgpt-prompt-pack.md`), and **(c)** the canonical gate's allowed-keys list *and* its new format-specific sub-checks (community_answer general-only; reel_script/caption banned-phrase; Discover title-honesty) to accept the new **optional** fields.
2. **THEN:** have `/draft-month` emit them inside the bundle.
3. **UNTIL that lands:** carry the new assets (pin angles, reel script, community answer) as a **separate companion file / clearly-marked addendum**, *never* as extra keys in the bundle the importer validates.

**What the engine drafts (automatable, $0 marginal on Max + capped Higgsfield credits):** all copy, all hook/pin variants, all stills, the clip, the virality score, the UTM links.
**What stays a manual human action (the guardrails reserve these for a person):** publishing, posting to Facebook groups, uploading Reels/Shorts, publishing/queuing Pinterest pins (no bulk automation), posting community answers, and the final review gate on *every* asset.

**One anti-slop rule governs the whole factory:** never stamp one template across many assets — YouTube's inauthentic-content rule and Pinterest's Gen-AI down-ranking both punish that at the *channel* level. Rotate the documented card/video axes (`{portrait|hands|object}` × gaze × time-of-day × accent color), require genuine human-added value per asset, space Pinterest pins ≥2 days per URL, and keep monthly Pinterest volume in the ~30–60 *reviewed* range so the bar holds.

## 6. The 30 / 60 / 90-day sequence with measurable checkpoints

> Checkpoints are read from tooling you already have — but **labeled by how you read them.** **[TOOL-READ]** = the engine surfaces it directly (Cockpit UTM clicks by campaign / per-group / per-variant; GSC Discover impressions). **[HAND-EST]** = you note it manually (GA4 pages/session per `utm_campaign`, per `docs/fb-click-playbook.md`; Reels→group-join deltas, which have no automatic attribution today). The north-star metric is never raw clicks — it's **engaged sessions and pages/session**, because that's what the revenue formula pays on.

### Days 0–30 — Land the contract, tighten the home base, plant the slow seed

**Do:**
- **Plumbing FIRST:** ship the one-time plugin/contract update (importer allowed-keys + Master Bundle Prompt + gate allowed-keys *and* the new sub-checks for `community_answer`, `reel_script`/caption, and Discover title-honesty). Nothing new is emitted into the bundle until this lands.
- **Day-0 blocker:** stand up the real-author bio + "general information, not medical advice" editorial page (before any Discover push).
- **1A:** Audit current group posts — confirm **zero captions contain URLs**; all links in first comment. Add one weekly engagement thread + one pinned evergreen per A-tier group.
- **1B:** Stand up the Pinterest business account, claim the domain, build 5–8 keyword boards. **Confirm the native scheduler's current queue limit.** Run **one `/draft-month`**, render the first batch of pin stills with Higgsfield, **queue what the native scheduler allows and hand-publish the rest — target ~30–60 reviewed pins/month, no bulk auto-poster.**
- **Discover groundwork:** start drip-publishing the month's cluster 2–3/week with honest `seo_title`s.

**Checkpoints (end of day 30):**
- ✅ Plugin contract update merged; the new optional fields validate through the importer and the gate (no "extra keys" rejection). **[TOOL-READ]**
- ✅ Editorial/author page live. **[TOOL-READ]**
- ✅ 100% of group posts: link in first comment, zero caption URLs. **[TOOL-READ via Cockpit logs]**
- ✅ Baseline captured: current group **pages/session + engaged-session rate** per A-tier group — your control number. **[HAND-EST from GA4]**
- ✅ Pinterest live, domain verified, first ~30 reviewed pins queued/published. **[TOOL-READ]**

### Days 31–60 — Add the reach funnel + let Discover get tested

**Do:**
- **2A:** With `reel_script` now gate-covered, render 12–16 Reels, **score every one with the Virality Predictor, ship only high-retention clips.** Hand-upload ~3/week. **Stand up the Reels-specific join proxy** (dedicated landing CTA and/or membership-delta log) so Reel-driven growth is at least hand-estimable.
- **2B:** Confirm the GSC Discover report is wired into `/retro`. Keep clusters interlinked and dripping.
- **1B:** Pinterest at steady reviewed cadence; start logging which **pin angle** sends the highest pages/session via `utm_content`.

**Checkpoints (end of day 60):**
- ✅ FB Page/group membership trend line is **up** **[TOOL-READ]**, with a measurable share plausibly tied to Reels via the proxy CTA / posting-day delta **[HAND-EST]** (the real Reels KPI — *not* views).
- ✅ Discover shows first **test-impressions** in GSC for at least one cluster. **[TOOL-READ]**
- ✅ `/retro` runs across both channels and can name a **winning hook angle** and a **winning pin angle** by pages/session. **[clicks TOOL-READ; pages/session HAND-EST]**
- ✅ Any group hook with high clicks but **low pages/session is killed** (broken payoff — matches the playbook's existing rule). **[HAND-EST]**

### Days 61–90 — Prove quality, prune ruthlessly, decide on Tier 3

**Do:**
- **1B:** Pinterest click volume should begin to build — verify Pinterest sessions show **higher pages/session** than the group baseline.
- **3A (optional):** Only if Tier 1–2 are stable *and* the `community_answer` gate sub-check is live, begin the Reddit/Quora **comment-only warm-up** on 3–5 chosen subs.

- ✅ **Pinterest pages/session ≥ group pages/session**, with outbound clicks trending up. **[clicks TOOL-READ; pages/session HAND-EST]**

- ✅ The whole month's cross-channel creative still came out of **one focused production session** — if it didn't, cut a channel, don't add hours.

## 7. The guardrails are the strategy (not a constraint on it)

Everything above lives *inside* the non-negotiables, and on these platforms that's not a tax — it's the moat:

- **General health facts only.** Every field of every bundle — article, pin, Reel script, **community answer**, **video caption**, overlay — passes the *same canonical `/guardrail-audit`*, **and the gate is actually extended to cover each new field before that field ships** (community_answer = general-only, no "you might have," includes the "a clinician is the right person for your specific case" note; reel_script/caption = same banned-phrase + general-only list as overlay_text, no symptom-as-self-diagnosis; Discover = page title must be the honest `seo_title`, never the FB hook). Body signals stay collective ("why most people feel X"), never personal ("is YOUR X a sign of…"). This is simultaneously the brand rule *and* the reach rule: **Pinterest's health-misinformation policy, YouTube's medical-misinformation policy, Meta's health/misinformation classifier, and TikTok's integrity policy** all bury the sensational/personal/miracle framing you're already built to avoid. Misinformation-flavored hooks may out-engage in the short run — and that is precisely the trap the guardrail exists to refuse.
- **No invented statistics — applied to ourselves too.** The platform figures in this doc are flagged **directional / verify-against-live-docs**, not quoted as settled fact. The decision-load-bearing ones (Pinterest payoff curve, Discover freshness window, link-in-comment reach, scheduler limits, AI-label rules) carry an explicit *"verify before committing the session"* note in §0. We hold our own claims to the standard we hold the health content to.
- **Manual posting, always — no bulk automation.** No bots, no bulk auto-posters, no schedulers touching Facebook groups; group/Reddit/Quora/Reels/Shorts actions are manual. **Pinterest is published/queued by hand too:** its *native in-product scheduled queue* may hold the few pins it allows, and the *approved Pinterest API path* is still "a deliberate click on reviewed content" — neither is unattended bulk automation, and the ~30–60-reviewed-pins cap keeps it that way.
- **Real human clicks only.** No bought views, no incentivized taps, no proxy traffic to any ad network. The UTM/GA4 loop measures *engaged* sessions precisely so you can tell a real Pinterest reader from a bouncing tap and never over-invest in the latter.
- **Review-first, every asset.** Claude drafts, Higgsfield renders and scores, the Virality Predictor ranks — and a human reviews and approves before *anything* publishes or posts. Automated write-backs are draft-only, and the importer/gate hard-reject anything that isn't a valid, reviewed bundle.
- **No scraping, public reading only.** Topic-demand signals come from *public* Reddit/Quora/Pinterest pages via web search — never logged-in or gated content.

**The one-line summary for the operator:** *Land the small contract/gate update first; then keep your groups warm, plant Pinterest now and let it compound (reviewing every pin, never bulk-automating), use Reels to grow the group while it's the only cold-start reach you have, let Discover fall out of the honest-titled articles you already write — and let one monthly session fan every article into all of it, with your hand on the publish button every time, and every platform number re-checked at the top of the session.*
